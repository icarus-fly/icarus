# 🏥 Traqa

**Your family's health, in words you understand.**

Traqa is an AI-powered health application designed for families across India. It bridges the gap between complex medical terminology and family understanding by transforming lab reports, prescriptions, and medical files into clear, accessible regional-language explanations.

---

## 🎯 Transforming Healthcare Literacy

For many families, receiving a medical report is an exercise in anxiety. Jargon-heavy terms, foreign units, and language barriers make it difficult to answer simple questions:
- *Is this report normal?*
- *What do these lab values mean for my family?*
- *What steps should we take next?*

Traqa solves this by translating complex medical documents into straightforward, culturally relevant summaries in the user's primary regional language.

---

## ✨ Core Concepts

### 1. Intelligent Report Simplification
* **Jargon Translation**: Automatically translates medical terms into simple, everyday descriptions.
* **Contextual Explanations**: Explains lab values and metrics relative to demographic averages and trends.
* **Multi-Language Accessibility**: Delivers insights in regional languages to support varying levels of English literacy.

### 2. Family Health Circles
* **Shared Health Dashboard**: Keeps family members connected with a unified view of historical reports and trends.
* **Consent-Based Access**: Allows users to manage permissions and sharing rules securely.

### 3. Regional Language First
* Designed to support the diverse linguistic landscape of India, featuring translation and localized voice playback in:
  * Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Assamese, Odia, Malayalam, Kannada, Telugu, Urdu, and English.
